1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement

India operates over 4,770+ Central and State welfare programs offering annual financial transfers, educational grants, healthcare cover, and business credit. However, despite enormous fiscal commitments, millions of eligible citizens—especially smallholder farmers, widows, rural artisans, students, and senior citizens—fail to access benefits due to:

  • Information Asymmetry: Schemes are dispersed across hundreds of separate ministries, state portals, and obscure eligibility criteria.
  • Language & Literacy Barriers: Complex administrative terminology and English-dominated portals exclude regional language speakers.
  • Redundant Multi-Page Applications: Citizens repeatedly fill identical demographic and identity attributes across cumbersome portal forms, leading to high abandonment rates and frequent data-entry errors.

Sahayak (सहायक) solves these foundational barriers through a unified, 3-tier architecture: (1) an accessible, flat 2D Custom DigiLocker Vault simulating verified credentials, (2) a high-concurrency Rust/Axum engine that matches dynamic demographic attributes against rulesets in milliseconds, and (3) a Chrome Manifest V3 Auto-Fill Extension with Levenshtein fuzzy DOM heuristics that populates official government application forms with one click.

Dynamic Attribute Editor & 2D Credential Simulator

The Vault includes a full-featured Attribute Editor that allows the PM/Tech Lead or citizen to toggle any demographic criteria (Age, Gender, Category, State) or socioeconomic factor (Annual Income, Landholding, PwD status, BPL status, Pregnancy, Girl Child Age). Changes update the 2D Document Simulator in real-time, rendering simulated verified certificates (Aadhaar, Tehsildar Income Certificate, SDM Caste Certificate, Land RoR/Khasra, and NFSA Ration Card).

6. Multilingual Bhashini AI Pipeline & Chrome MV3 Auto-Fill

A. End-to-End Language Mirroring

The platform dynamically preserves and mirrors the citizen's language end-to-end: Input Audio (Hindi/Tamil/etc.) ➔ ASR ➔ Multilingual Engine ➔ TTS Audio (Mirrored Language). Devanagari and regional scripts are fully rendered using Google Fonts (Noto Sans Devanagari, Rozha One).

B. Chrome Manifest V3 Auto-Fill Engine

  • Fuzzy DOM Heuristics: Normalized Levenshtein distance and semantic dictionaries map form fields (aadhaar, income_cert, khasra_no, father_name, dob, etc.) even when form IDs differ across state portals.
  • Synthetic Event Dispatch: Emits synthetic input, change, and blur events to satisfy modern reactive frameworks (React, Angular, Vue, ASP.NET).
  • Cross-Context Bridge: Coordinates through extensionBridge.js using window.postMessage and CustomEvents with seamless local session caching.

7. Strict Data Privacy & Zero Realistic PII Restraint

To comply with government data governance standards and prevent simulation of real citizen credentials:

  • Aadhaar Numbers: Strictly output as XXXX-XXXX-XXXX or [Aadhaar Redacted]. Zero realistic numeric digits are ever generated.
  • Phone Numbers: Strictly output as [Phone Redacted].
  • Certificate Numbers: Strictly formatted with placeholder tokens, e.g., UP/REV/INC/XXXX/XXXXXX or [ID-Omitted].
